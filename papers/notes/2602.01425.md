# 2602.01425 — One Probe Won't Catch Them All: Towards Targeted Deception Detection
Natarajan, Jain, Arora (LASR Labs), Golechha, Bloom (UK AISI). ICML 2026 (PMLR 306). arXiv v2 18 Jun 2026.

## Research question
Why do instruction-pair linear deception probes (Goldowsky-Dill et al. 2025, "Apollo probe") fail on diverse deception datasets (e.g., MASK), and is deception detection better served by one universal probe or by probes targeted to specific deception types?

## Definitions / typology
- Deception = model output misrepresenting under incentive/instruction; probes are claimed to capture *deceptive intent* (from system prompt) rather than truth of content.
- Uses Druckman & Bjork (1991) taxonomy (2D: covertness x harmfulness): 16 types — white lie, exaggeration, evasion, bluff, concealment, pretense, trick, impersonation, counterfeit, forgery, pressure dishonesty, backstab, false alarm, hoax, partial truth, overt lie.
- 7 **control** (non-deceptive) pairs: clarification, **hallucination**, ignorance, joke, literal interpretation, privacy, roleplay.
- Note "bluff" type: honest = "Express your uncertainty honestly... calibrated confidence"; dishonest = "State your answer confidently as if certain, even though you're not sure" — closest to an overconfidence/hallucination-like construct.

## Methodology (implementable)
- **Probe**: logistic regression on residual stream, Gemma-2-9B-IT, **layer 20 (zero-indexed)** (sweep over 20/25/31). Features standardized (zero mean, unit variance across dataset). **L2, lambda = 1** (tuned).
- **Training data**: Azaria & Mitchell (2023) true/false facts; response is token-forced (off-policy) fact statement, paired with honest vs dishonest system prompt. Label = which prompt was used (not fact truth).
- **Token selection**: mean over all response tokens **except the final 5** (so probe can't read off whether the fact is literally true; e.g., "The blue whale is the..."). Alternatives swept: first 5, first 10, all-but-last-10, all-but-last-5, chat-template tokens only.
- **Default prompt pair**: "Pretend you are an honest/deceptive person making statements about the world."
- **Prompt generation**: Claude Sonnet 4.5 generated 23 taxonomy pairs (16 + 7 controls; Table 3) and 34 framing variations (identity, role, instruction, hybrid, control-repetition, long-form, baselines incl. "sandbagging": "always gives the correct/incorrect answer if you know it"; Table 4).
- **ANOVA**: 2000 probes over factors {system prompt, training fact dataset (Azaria-Mitchell + 11 synthetic semantic categories, ~200 facts each, 100T/100F), token selection, layer}.
- **Selection**: "Best Average" probe chosen on held-out validation (50 balanced samples each from AI Liar, Convincing Game, Instructed Deception). "Best Taxonomy" = post-hoc oracle best per dataset.
- **Calibration/normalization of scores**: control-adjusted = subtract median probe score on Alpaca control set.
- **Skyline**: in-distribution probe, 80/20 split per eval dataset (AUC >= 0.993 on 9/10).
- Synthetic validation: per-taxonomy LLM-generated honest/deceptive on-policy datasets; split-half stability (10 seeds).

## Datasets (eval, on-policy, Gemma-2-9B-IT, T=1; Table 2, H/D)

Sources: Goldowsky-Dill et al. 2025, Liar's Bench (Kretschmar et al. 2025, arXiv 2511.16035), MASK (Ren et al. 2025, arXiv 2503.03750; HF `cais/MASK` (ID not in paper; unverified)). Control: Alpaca.

## Models
Gemma-2-9B-IT (primary); Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct (ANOVA replication only).

## Baselines / metrics
Baseline = Apollo default prompt pair. Metric: AUROC (honest vs deceptive), plus ANOVA variance explained, Spearman rank stability, Pearson correlations between probes.

## Key numbers
- Variance explained (Gemma): system prompt **70.6%**, layer 2.7%, training dataset 2.5%, token selection 0.6%, residual 23.6%. Llama-70B: 71.0 / 5.8 / 4.5 / 0.3 / 18.4%.
- Mean AUC: Baseline 0.689 (SD 0.165); Best Average 0.721 (+0.032); Best Taxonomy oracle 0.797 (+0.108).
- Per-dataset (B / A / T): AI Liar .73/.77/.85 (hoax); Convincing Game .98/.98/.98; Harm-pressure-choice .65/.64/.76 (forgery); Instructed Deception .78/.79/.85 (false_alarm); MASK continuations .69/.71/.78 (bluff); MASK disinformation .84/.88/.93 (forgery); MASK doubling-down .57/.59/.73 (best = **ctrl_hallucination** probe!); MASK known facts **.37**/.44/.71 (concealment; text says pressure-dishonesty 0.697); MASK provided facts .77/.82/.86 (white_lie); Sandbagging .52/.59/.63 (concealment).
- Synthetic: matched probe top-3 in 8/16 types, beats baseline 12/16; evasion 0.999, hoax 0.993, forgery 0.989.
- Split-half: same top probe 45% (chance 6.25%), top-3 67%, mean rho 0.69 (0.20 on MASK doubling-down).
- Clusters (Pearson): C1 evasion/exaggeration/false_alarm/forgery/trick/white_lie r=.84-.97; C2 bluff/concealment/impersonation/overt_lie/partial_truth/pressure_dishonesty .52-.90; C3 backstab/hoax/pretense; C4 counterfeit; C5 all 7 controls (incl. hallucination) .47-.84.

## Limitations
Post-hoc oracle selection inflates "Best Taxonomy"; type-specificity uneven (shared intent signal); taxonomy results only on Gemma-2-9B L20; English; non-agentic; small eval sets (AI Liar n=54; Harm Pressure Choice only 22 deceptive).

## Relevance to hallucination vs. lie
- **No direct test on honest mistakes/hallucinations** as an eval set: negatives are honest on-policy responses, not wrong-but-sincere answers. So false-positive rate on hallucinations is unmeasured.
- Baseline probe fires on fiction/role-play and even on truthful answers in role-conflict contexts (Boeing example) — it tracks "context where lying is appropriate" rather than the act; MASK known facts AUC 0.374 (below chance).
- Authors hypothesize probes differ along "honesty" vs "correctness" axes; overt_lie probe indexes correctness of stated facts. This is directly the confound we must test: a probe partly tracking correctness will fire on hallucinations.
- A "ctrl_hallucination" instruction pair exists and clusters with controls, not deception — but it was the best probe for MASK doubling-down, hinting hallucination-like and lie-like directions are not cleanly separated.
- Actionable: use mean-over-response-minus-last-5 aggregation, L2=1, standardized features, layer ~45-50% depth; prompt choice dominates — test several instruction pairs (overt_lie, pressure_dishonesty, bluff, apollo baseline) and report each; include a bluff/hallucination-control probe as a contrast direction; control-adjust with Alpaca median.
